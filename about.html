About

Background, training, and the approach behind the research on this site.

Positioning

I am an economist and policy researcher with a background in macroeconomics, monetary policy, and international relations theory. My work uses econometric methods, principally VAR and VECM modeling in Stata and EViews, alongside structured qualitative methods, including codebook thematic analysis and AI-assisted research workflows, to study how monetary policy moves through Pakistan’s economy and how the institutions that set it explain their own reasoning.

My current research centers on monetary policy transmission and central bank communication, extending toward industrial heterogeneity, and, over time, toward the wider technological, geopolitical, and maritime forces that shape how policy is made and transmitted.

Open to

Research-oriented roles in economic consulting, central banking, international organizations, and think tanks, and exploring PhD opportunities in Economics. Open to remote, onsite, and hybrid work, worldwide.
