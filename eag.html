Economic Advisory Group

My current institutional affiliation, and the source of my ongoing work on central bank communication.

Associate Member, Economic Advisory Group (EAG)

April 2026 – March 2027

The Economic Advisory Group is a research and policy programme run in partnership with the PRIME Institute. As an Associate Member, I work with an assigned mentor on a year-long, mentored research output, with four policy briefs expected across the membership term. The first, a thematic analysis of SBP Monetary Policy Committee statements, is complete and under review (see Diagnosing Inflation).

Prior selection: EAG Associate Programme, 2025 Cohort

Before the current membership term, I was competitively selected, nationally, for the EAG Associate Programme’s 2025 Cohort, run jointly with the PRIME Institute. The cohort included workshops on monetary policy, capital flows, and debt sustainability This selection is distinct from, and precedes, the current paid membership term.
