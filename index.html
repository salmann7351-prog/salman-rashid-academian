02Research interests

Research Interests

01

Macroeconomics & Monetary Policy

The core of my current work is monetary transmission and central-bank communication. I am extending this work toward advanced macroeconomic modelling, including DSGE and heterogeneous-agent frameworks such as HANK, to study policy transmission, distributional effects, and the interaction between monetary policy and real economic behaviour.

02

AI, Technology & Labour

The economic effects of artificial intelligence on productivity, employment, labour demand, research practices, and economic decision-making, with particular interest in AI adoption and the changing economics of knowledge work.

03

Political Economy & Institutions

How institutions, incentives, social cohesion, and state capacity shape economic behaviour and development; with particular interest in conflict, political violence, household responses to insecurity, and the institutional foundations of economic decline.

04

Maritime Economics & Geoeconomics

How geopolitical conflict and disruption affect trade routes, shipping, exchange rates, imported inputs, production costs, and macroeconomic stability.

03Evidence

Research that shows its work.

Figures and empirical results are presented as part of the argument, not as decoration. My current work focuses on monetary transmission, central-bank communication, and heterogeneity across Pakistan’s manufacturing industries.
